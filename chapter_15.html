Mechanical Workshop

You are the Safety Manager. Walk the shop floor, examine every machine, and decide whether each one is a mechanical hazard or compliant.

Move with WASD or arrow keys (hold Shift to run) or click the floor. Press E or Space near an object, or click it, to inspect. Zoom with the wheel, + / - keys or the slider. Yellow ? markers show inspection points.
