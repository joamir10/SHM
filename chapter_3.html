Investigation Facility

You are the Safety Manager Engineer. Enter through the main door, investigate nine zones, collect evidence, and classify every finding as an unsafe act, unsafe condition, workload factor, management failure or effective control.

Move with WASD or arrows (Shift to run) or click the floor. Press E, Space or Enter near an object, or click it. Zoom with the wheel or + and -. Esc closes the panel.
